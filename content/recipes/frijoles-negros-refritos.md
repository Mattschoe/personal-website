ingredients:
  - amount: 200 g
    item: dried black beans
  - amount: 2
    item: onion
  - amount: 4
    item: garlic cloves
  - amount: 2 tsp
    item: cumin
  - amount: 2 tsp
    item: oregano
steps:
  - Soak the beans in water and hydrate them in the fridge for about 24 hours.
  - Dice onion and garlic fine and cook them in a bit of olive oil
  - Add spices and toast them in the pan
  - Add beans and enough bean water to cover about 1 cm above the beans. Cook the liquid down over a few hours. You can check the consistency by taking a spoon and cooling it down a bit, the beans will get much thicker when cooled.
  - When the dish has reached a good consistency (think chunky salsa thickness) take it off the heat and add an acid, I recommend apple cider vinegar, to your liking together with salt and pepper.
